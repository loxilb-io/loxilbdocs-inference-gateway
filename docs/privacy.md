# Documentation analytics preferences

Reading these docs does not require analytics. Choose **Reject analytics** to keep
Google Analytics disabled, or **Accept analytics** to help us understand which
public documentation pages people use. You can reopen **Analytics preferences**
at the bottom of any page and withdraw consent.

Before you accept, this integration sends no requests to Google Analytics or
Google Tag Manager. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals keep
analytics disabled. Local previews and forks do not send analytics events.

After acceptance, Google Analytics receives page-view events for this documentation
site. The integration supplies the canonical document URL without its query string
or fragment, the document title, and the referring site's origin without its path
or query. Google Analytics can set session cookies and receives connection metadata
as part of serving the analytics request. We do not export visitor-level events into
the project's reporting repository. See [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
for Google's processing of analytics data.

The site stores your analytics choice in your browser's local storage. Withdrawing
consent disables further collection by this integration and clears its own analytics
cookies on the documentation path. Previously received data is not removed by a
browser preference change. Blocking cookies, scripts, or analytics can limit the
statistics; absence of measured activity does not prove absence of visitors.

The integration does not send search terms, form values, full referring URLs,
link targets, or arbitrary URL parameters. Automated enhanced measurement is
disabled for this stream. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled.
